Claude Gmail

A private, single-user tool.

Claude Gmail is a personal tool built and used by one person, Jayden Leung, to read, summarize, search, draft and organize email in his own Gmail accounts, and to view and manage his own Google Calendar, with the help of an AI assistant (Claude).

It is not offered to the public and has no other users. It only ever accesses the three Google accounts that belong to its owner.
